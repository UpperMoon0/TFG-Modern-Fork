# TFG 0.13.19-nstut.12 — Clockwork industrial progression and renewable Wanderlite

Integrates #100 and #101 from the current stable .11 release without changing the existing mod pins or prior immutable tags.

- Natural deep Wanderlite ore in twenty TFC stone hosts; production rarity 420.
- Seed-free Steam bootstrap: sixteen Greate mixing batches and 4x4 mechanical crafting, consuming 64 raw graphite, 64 normal magnetite and 16 amethyst shards per first crystal.
- Steam through EV industrial Clockwork recipes, including pre-electric Physics Infuser crafting.
- HV seed-gated ore synthesis: mixer, chemical reactor and autoclave, reusable vitrified pearls and crystal seed, 1,440,000 EU per ore.
- Source validators and isolated Forge acceptance tools. Test-only fixtures and rarity overlay must never enter a production installation.

Release requires native gameplay evidence, signed Jenkins policy/promotion and release acceptance, and an offline-player deployment with rollback retained.
